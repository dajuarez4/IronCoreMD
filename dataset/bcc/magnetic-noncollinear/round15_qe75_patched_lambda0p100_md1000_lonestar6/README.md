# Round 15: Round-11 setup extended to 1000 MD steps

## Purpose

Round 15 repeats the successful Round-11 eight-atom noncollinear calculation
with `nstep = 1000` (approximately 1 ps at `dt = 20.67` QE/Rydberg atomic-time
units). It is a fresh run from the same Round-11 positions, velocities, spin
directions, SCF ramp, and thermostat settings; it does not reuse or modify the
completed Round-11 restart directory.

The longer trajectory is intended to test whether the stable electronic and
local-moment behavior observed through 400 steps persists and whether the
temperature approaches a bounded stationary regime. The eight-atom cell is
still a convergence/stability diagnostic, not a production-size thermodynamic
sample.

## Fixed design inherited from Round 11

- BCC Fe eight-atom `2x2x1` cell, `a = 2.39 angstrom`;
- eight separately labeled Fe species (`Fe01`--`Fe08`);
- `ntyp = 8`, safely below the current QE build limit of 10 species;
- thermally displaced starting positions and seed-`400054` 4000 K velocities;
- noncollinear atomic target of `2 Bohr magneton/Fe`;
- private patched QE 7.5 atomic constraint;
- fresh constraint ramp `lambda = 0.005 -> 0.020 -> 0.100 Ry`;
- SCF thresholds `4e-3 -> 1e-3 -> 3e-4 Ry`;
- MD `conv_thr = 4e-3 Ry`, `electron_maxstep = 500`;
- Gamma point, Fermi-Dirac smearing, `degauss = 0.025334 Ry`;
- SVR thermostat at 4000 K with `nraise = 20`;
- 1000 MD steps with `dt = 20.67`.

The generator and submission script refuse to overwrite an existing result or
`qe_tmp` directory. Do not copy the Round-11 `qe_tmp` directory into Round 15.

## Acceptance criteria

A technically complete result must:

1. contain exactly 1000 `Entering Dynamics` records and `JOB DONE`;
2. contain no failed SCF, QE routine, Cholesky, or `cdiaghg` error;
3. keep converged MD cycles below `conv_thr = 4e-3 Ry`;
4. avoid cycles approaching `electron_maxstep = 500`;
5. retain a trailing mean local moment preferably above `1 Bohr magneton/Fe`;
6. show bounded temperature, force, pressure, energy, and moment histories.

Treat at least the initial 100 configurations as equilibration. Because Round
11 averaged only about 2945 K rather than the requested 4000 K, do not call
Round 15 an equilibrated 4000 K trajectory unless its longer trailing
temperature statistics support that conclusion.

## Completed result checked 2026-08-19

The returned `md_1000steps.out` is complete. Its SHA-256 is
`06c414417a99469aa0180f77942ad59dda6b51d1548995475a420b7a67725a81`.
QE ran on eight MPI tasks and reached exactly 1000 `Entering Dynamics`
records, 1000 converged MD SCFs, the maximum-step termination message, and
`JOB DONE`. The reported PWSCF wall time was 6 h 9 min. There were no
`convergence NOT achieved`, QE-routine, Cholesky, or `cdiaghg` failures.

Electronic convergence remained stable:

- mean electronic iterations per MD SCF: `6.254`;
- maximum electronic iterations: `28`;
- mean final estimated accuracy: `2.879e-3 Ry`;
- maximum final estimated accuracy: `3.99807e-3 Ry`, below the `4e-3 Ry`
  threshold;
- recoverable `c_bands` nonconvergence warnings: `491`.

The local moments remained robust. The trajectory mean was
`1.60703 Bohr magneton/Fe`, the final-100 mean was `1.61756`, and individual
printed magnitudes ranged from `1.46303` to `1.77620`. The largest local-spin
direction change from the initial frame was only `4.33 degrees`, consistent
with the full-vector atomic constraint at `lambda=0.10 Ry`.

The time-averaged total-magnetization vector was
`(-0.00015, +0.05564, -0.00137) Bohr magneton/cell`, with norm
`0.05566 Bohr magneton/cell` or `0.00696 Bohr magneton/Fe`. Over the final 500
steps its vector-mean norm was smaller, `0.01587 Bohr magneton/cell` or
`0.00198 Bohr magneton/Fe`. This is consistent with a constrained
disordered-local-moment state, but it does not demonstrate freely rotating or
ergodically decorrelated spins.

The thermostat did not establish a 4000 K trajectory. Including all 1001
printed ionic-temperature records, the mean was `2736.54 K`; the final-200 and
final-100 means were `2402.47 K` and `2411.17 K`, respectively. The final
instantaneous value was `3320.06 K`. The 100-step block means ranged from
approximately `2117 K` to `3920 K`, so the temperature remained strongly
oscillatory and its late-time mean was far below the target.

The structure remained bounded during this short trajectory: the minimum
periodic Fe--Fe separation was `1.679 A`, the maximum RMS displacement from
the ideal sites was `0.424 A`, and the final RMS displacement was `0.374 A`.
The mean pressure was `3770.17 kbar` (`377.02 GPa`), with values from
`3572.64` to `4023.43 kbar`.

Force quality is the main numerical limitation. QE printed `SCF correction
compared to forces is large` in all 1000 steps. The mean ratio of printed SCF
force correction to total force was `0.998`; it exceeded one in 447 steps and
exceeded 0.5 in 881 steps. Consequently, the trajectory is suitable as an
electronic/magnetic and structural-stability test, but its existing forces
are not suitable for quantitative TDEP or phonon fitting. Selected
decorrelated configurations would need new static force calculations at a
substantially tighter SCF threshold.

Acceptance summary: completion, electronic convergence, local-moment
retention, small net magnetization, and bounded structure pass. Equilibration
near 4000 K and force-quality requirements fail. Only the MD output was
returned at the round root; the three SCF-stage outputs were not returned, so
their detailed histories cannot be independently audited from this local
copy, although the submitted workflow starts MD only after verifying those
stages converged.
