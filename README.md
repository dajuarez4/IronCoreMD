# IronCoreMD

First-principles iron datasets for high-pressure, high-temperature studies: BCC, FCC and HCP trajectories from Quantum ESPRESSO, finite-temperature phonons from TDEP/HELD, and archives for machine-learning workflows.

### ATLAS-Fe: simulation coverage

The ATLAS-Fe inventory combines the local `dataset/` archives and removes byte-identical archives and matching fixed-order frames. It retains **98,577 configurations** across BCC (70,195), FCC (20,003), and HCP (8,379); PCA omitted two configurations with incomplete selected descriptors. The coverage includes BCC noncollinear sampling (1,545 configurations in 20 archives) that is not part of the default nonmagnetic ML selectors.

</p>

<p align="center">

</p>

<p align="center">

</p>

The atlas makes coverage gaps visible: HCP is sampled over a much narrower volume range (6.42–7.17 Å³/atom) than BCC (5.79–14.19) or FCC (5.79–11.66); retained HCP frames span roughly 3,048–6,752 K and 249–426 GPa. The combined inventory also has no FCC or HCP magnetic trajectories, and BCC noncollinear coverage is much smaller than BCC nonmagnetic coverage. These are dataset coverage observations, not equilibrium phase boundaries. PCA uses standardized volume, energy, temperature, pressure, and force RMS descriptors; it is a visualization, not a stability metric.

Figures and count tables are generated by the external [ATLAS-Fe notebook](../dataset_atlas/iron_dataset_atlas.ipynb); raw NPZ trajectories remain the source of record. See [dataset policy and selectors](dataset/README.md).

### Round25: constrained noncollinear BCC

![Round25 phonons and MD](docs/round25/round25_dashboard.png)

Archived September 21 analysis: 32 Fe, 4000 K target, 92 fitted configurations. Ensemble TDEP and mean HELD have no imaginary modes on the sampled path; some individual frames do. [Animation and methods](docs/round25/README.md).

![Round25 spin entropy estimate](docs/round25/round25_spin_entropy.png)

Independent-local-moment estimate: **0.95643 kB/Fe**. Constrained-spin model estimate, not measured equilibrium entropy; not added to Helmholtz free energies.
